Engineering capabilities

Custom Analog Layout

Develop custom analog and mixed-signal circuits at transistor level, balancing performance, power, and area for your target process.

Your design challenge

Analog layout around your requirements.

Analog blocks rarely drop in unchanged from one process to the next. Our team designs and characterizes at transistor level so PLLs, converters, and power circuits hit spec on your target node.

Capabilities

  • PLL & clock generation design
  • High-speed ADC/DAC design
  • LDO & power management circuits
  • Sensor interface & analog front-end design
  • Transistor-level layout & parasitic extraction

Project outcomes

What you get

  • Schematic & layout deliverables
  • Custom analog IP blocks
  • Process characterization data
  • Corner & Monte Carlo simulation report
  • Post-silicon bring-up support

Deliverables and support are agreed against your project scope.
