  • About the Role

    V-Chip is seeking a Custom Layout Engineer to develop transistor-level IC layouts for analog and mixed-signal circuits, working closely with our analog design and verification teams to deliver silicon-ready layouts on advanced process nodes.

    Responsibilities

    • Develop and optimize custom IC layouts for analog and mixed-signal blocks such as op-amps, bandgaps, and current mirrors
    • Apply CMOS process knowledge and IC layout principles to meet performance, matching, and reliability targets
    • Build and maintain layouts using Cadence Virtuoso or Synopsys Custom Compiler
    • Run and resolve DRC/LVS and physical verification checks (Calibre or ICV)
    • Develop scripts in SKILL, Python, or Tcl to automate and improve layout efficiency
    • Collaborate with analog designers and verification engineers to close out layout-dependent effects

    Qualifications

    • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Electrical Engineering or related field
    • Basic understanding of CMOS process and IC layout principles
    • Familiar with Cadence Virtuoso/Synopsys Custom Compiler layout tool
    • Exposure to DRC/LVS and physical verification flows (Calibre or ICV preferred)
    • Scripting basics: familiar with SKILL, Python, or Tcl for layout automation and efficiency improvements
    • Familiarity with analog building blocks such as op-amps, bandgaps, and current mirrors
    • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
    • Excellent verbal and written communication skills
    • Strong interpersonal skills and ability to collaborate across global teams

  • About the Role

    V-Chip is seeking a hands-on Lead Design Verification Engineer to lead pre-silicon verification of complex semiconductor IP, subsystems, and SoC designs. You will define verification strategies, guide execution, and collaborate with multidisciplinary engineering teams to deliver robust, production-ready silicon.

    Responsibilities

    • Own the verification strategy, methodology, schedule, and sign-off criteria for assigned IP, subsystem, or full-chip projects
    • Develop comprehensive, requirements-driven verification plans and ensure traceability from specifications to tests and coverage
    • Architect and develop reusable SystemVerilog/UVM verification environments, including testbenches, sequences, agents, scoreboards, reference models, assertions, and coverage models
    • Lead constrained-random, directed, assertion-based, and coverage-driven verification activities
    • Execute and debug RTL and gate-level simulations, regressions, and failing test cases
    • Drive verification closure by analysing functional and code coverage, identifying gaps, and coordinating corrective actions
    • Review design specifications and RTL implementations to identify functional, integration, and verification risks early
    • Collaborate closely with architects, RTL designers, software engineers, physical design engineers, DFT engineers, and external IP providers
    • Support design integration, logic synthesis, linting, clock-domain crossing checks, reset-domain checks, and formal equivalence activities where required
    • Establish verification best practices, reusable infrastructure, automation, and quality metrics across projects
    • Provide technical leadership, conduct reviews, mentor junior engineers, and support project planning and resource estimation
    • Communicate verification status, risks, dependencies, and sign-off readiness clearly to project stakeholders

    Qualifications

    • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Electrical Engineering, Electronics Engineering, Computer Engineering, or a related discipline
    • At least 7 years of relevant experience in ASIC, SoC, FPGA, or IP-level functional verification, including technical leadership responsibilities
    • Strong hands-on experience with SystemVerilog and UVM
    • Proven experience developing verification plans and coverage-driven verification environments from the ground up
    • Solid understanding of constrained-random verification, functional coverage, code coverage, assertions, and verification closure
    • Strong debugging skills across testbench, RTL, waveform, protocol, and system-level failures
    • Experience with industry-standard simulation and verification tools from Cadence, Siemens EDA, or Synopsys
    • Familiarity with common interfaces and protocols such as AMBA AXI/AHB/APB, PCIe, Ethernet, DDR, USB, or other high-speed interfaces
    • Good understanding of digital design concepts, SoC architectures, clock and reset structures, and hardware/software interactions
    • Working knowledge of scripting or programming languages such as Python, Perl, Tcl, C, or C++
    • Strong communication, problem-solving, technical leadership, and cross-functional collaboration skills

    Preferred Experience

    • Verification of complex SoCs, processor-based systems, high-speed interfaces, or reusable semiconductor IP
    • Formal verification, emulation, FPGA prototyping, low-power verification, or performance verification
    • Gate-level simulation, logic synthesis, lint, CDC/RDC analysis, and equivalence checking
    • Regression automation, continuous integration, version-control workflows, and verification productivity tools
    • Experience mentoring engineers or leading a verification team through project sign-off

  • About the Role

    V-Chip is seeking a Physical Design Engineer to own block- and full-chip-level physical implementation, from floorplan through GDSII signoff, on advanced process nodes.

    Responsibilities

    • Own floorplanning, place-and-route, timing closure, and power/clock optimization for blocks or full chips
    • Drive designs from architecture through GDSII, taking ownership of multiple complex projects in parallel
    • Apply SoC/ASIC design methodologies and tools across the physical implementation flow
    • Support physical design signoff, working from RTL through to final layout
    • Develop and maintain scripts in TCL, Perl, or Python to automate and improve implementation flows
    • Collaborate with RTL, verification, and DFT teams to resolve cross-domain implementation issues

    Qualifications

    • Bachelor's degree or higher in EE, CS or related field
    • Experience in floorplan, P&R, timing closure, clock, power, and design optimization on advanced nodes
    • Experience with SoC/ASIC design methodologies and tools
    • Full chip or block level ownership from architecture to GDSII, driving multiple complex designs to production
    • Basic understanding of RTL design (Verilog / VHDL)
    • Familiarity with physical design signoff requirements and flows
    • Experience with Cadence and/or Synopsys physical design tools/flows
    • Proficiency in scripting skills (TCL, Perl, Python)
    • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
    • Excellent verbal and written communication skills
    • Strong interpersonal skills and ability to collaborate across global teams
