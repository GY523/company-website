Who we are

Specialist by discipline.
Connected by design.

V-Chip is an IC design service company providing end-to-end solutions across the semiconductor development lifecycle. Our expertise spans architecture planning, IP selection, design verification, custom analog layout, and physical design, supporting customers from initial concept through to silicon.

As we continue to expand our team, V-Chip is taking on increasingly complex and higher-value IC design projects while contributing to the growth of Malaysia’s semiconductor design ecosystem.

Where to find us

Our IC design facility opened in September 2025, purpose-built to bring architecture, IP, verification, analog, and physical design capability together under one roof.
