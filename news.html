Team Spotlight

From Learning to Engineering: Three Journeys at V-Chip

Penang, Malaysia | 14 September 2026

Every engineer starts somewhere. For Saiful, Aznur and Raja, joining V-Chip meant three very different paths into semiconductor engineering.

Returning to the Industry

After nearly two years in the automotive industry, Saiful joined V-Chip and progressed from trainee to permanent staff.

Learning Backend From Scratch

Aznur gained hands-on exposure to backend and analog layout work, including a bandgap reference layout and DRC/LVS verification. She now continues as an Analog Layout Engineer.

Mastering Custom Layout

Custom layout was entirely new to Raja. Through practical training, he learned to read schematics and build layouts — capped by optimizing his own full-adder design.

Three different starting points, one shared experience of learning and growing at V-Chip. Hear their stories in their own words. 🎥

Training Milestone

OJT Trainees Complete Their V-Chip Journey

Penang, Malaysia | 14 August 2026

Congratulations to our latest batch of OJT trainees on completing their training journey — leaving with real, hands-on IC design experience across Physical Design and Analog Layout.

Three Months of Hands-On Experience

The programme began with one month of technical training under the Advanced Semiconductor Academy of Malaysia (ASEM), followed by three months of practical engineering work at V-Chip in Penang, supported by Khazanah Nasional Berhad through the K-Youth initiative. Working closely with V-Chip engineers, trainees took part directly in real design and problem-solving.

“Opportunity, Exposure, Environment”

That’s how one trainee summed up the experience. Beyond the technical work, the cohort became part of the V-Chip community — sharing long hours and memorable moments with colleagues, including team members from China.

Congratulations on completing an important chapter of your engineering journey — we look forward to seeing what you achieve next.

Regional Partnership

Building Connections Across Asia: V-Chip Visits Bangladesh

Dhaka, Bangladesh | 21 April 2026

V-Chip extends its sincere appreciation to Siliconova and Neural Semiconductor for their hospitality during our recent visit to Bangladesh, strengthening ties across Asia’s semiconductor ecosystem.

Sharing Vision and Expertise

Our hosts generously shared their company journey, technical capabilities and vision for the future — valuable insight into the innovation and dedication driving their work.

Strengthening Regional Ties

Beyond the exchange of knowledge, the visit strengthened relationships between semiconductor teams across Asia. V-Chip looks forward to staying connected and exploring future collaboration.
